bio: Carlos Baquero is a Professor in the Department of Informatics Engineering (DEI) at FEUP, University of Porto, and area coordinator at the High-Assurance Software Laboratory (HASLab) within INESC TEC. His research centres on data management in eventually consistent settings, distributed data aggregation, and causality tracking, and he is one of the co-creators of Conflict-Free Replicated Data Types (CRDTs). Alongside CRDTs, his work on mechanisms such as Scalable Bloom Filters, Interval Tree Clocks, and Dotted Version Vectors has been adopted in widely used systems including Riak, Redis, Akka distributed data, and Azure Cosmos DB.
