bio: Guido Salvaneschi is a Full Professor at the School of Computer Science, University of St. Gallen, Switzerland, where he leads the Software and Systems Security Group. His research lies at the intersection of programming languages, software engineering, and cybersecurity, and draws on methods from software verification, formal methods, software testing, and programming language design. He holds a PhD in Information Technology from Politecnico di Milano, Italy, and was previously an Assistant Professor at the Technical University of Darmstadt, Germany. His research has been supported, among others, by the German Research Foundation (DFG) and the Swiss National Science Foundation (SNSF).
