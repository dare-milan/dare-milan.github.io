bio: Claudia-Lavinia Ignat is a senior research scientist at Inria and head of the LORELEY team. She received her PhD in Computer Science from ETH Zurich, Switzerland, and her habilitation from the University of Lorraine. Her research focuses on distributed collaborative systems that support group work across distance. Her work combines distributed systems and computer-supported cooperative work, with particular attention to collaborative data management, including replication mechanisms, security in decentralized environments, and trustworthy collaboration. She served as General Co-Chair and Program Committee Co-Chair of ECSCW 2018, is an Associate Editor of the Journal of Computer Supported Cooperative Work, and regularly serves as an editor for CSCW conferences and on the program committees of major conferences including CHI, ECSCW, Middleware, and SRDS. She has also coordinated several national, international, and industrial research projects, including the USCOAST Inria associated team, the Alvearium Inria Challenge project, the IPCEI DXP project, and the PILOT project within PEPR eNSEMBLE.
