bio: Dr. Claudia-Lavinia Ignat is a senior research scientist at Inria in Nancy, France and the head of Loreley team. She obtained a PhD in Computer Science from ETH Zurich, Switzerland in 2006 and an Habilitation to Direct Researches (HDR) from Lorraine University, France in 2021. Her research interests are distributed collaborative systems with a focus on consistency maintenance, group awareness, security, trust issues and user studies. She is in the editorial board of Journal of CSCW and she is program committee member of several international conferences such as Middleware, CHI, CSCW, ECSCW, GROUP, CollabTech, CDVE and ICCP. She coordinates several research and industrial projects and she was the coordinator of the USCOAST associated Inria team.
